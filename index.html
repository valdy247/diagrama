09 / GOOD QUESTIONS

A little
more clarity.

Do I need a separate app for every tool?

No. The vision is one personalized VMV Business app with the modules your company needs, connected to a web platform for advanced management.

How does translation fit into Smart Call?

Smart Call is designed around a simple phone experience. Supported bilingual calls can use live interpretation, while advanced language and line settings belong in the web platform.

What's handled in the app versus the web?

Everyday work — calls, appointments, customer lookups and inventory checks — belongs in the app. Setup, website editing, integrations and complex workflows belong on the web. Advanced options can be opened from the app.

Can the workspace change as my business grows?

That's the idea: businesses should be able to add modules and organize their workspace without switching to a completely different platform.

Are all of these products available today?

This website illustrates VMV Business's product direction. Some solutions are being developed or planned. Availability, pricing and integrations will be announced when they are verified.
